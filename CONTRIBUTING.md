# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through [Issues](https://github.com/gusgustavodj/kontrol-s8-mixxx-mappings/issues) and [Pull Requests](https://github.com/gusgustavodj/kontrol-s8-mixxx-mappings/pulls). Use Issues for bugs, mapping improvements, compatibility reports, or additional platform requests. State operating system/version, Mixxx revision, device/hardware revision if known, dependencies, limitations and what you tested. Do not upload private information, proprietary captures, or material you lack rights to publish.

Pull Requests should explain the proposed change, motivation, affected files, tests/evidence, relevant Issue, and licensing or third-party material considerations. Keep platform-specific files in the appropriate platform directory; do not claim support until implementation and validation are documented.

Contributions are welcome. All pull requests are reviewed by the maintainer, who decides whether changes are incorporated into the official repository. Issues and Pull Requests do not obligate implementation, acceptance, or merging. The maintainer may request changes, accept a proposal, or close it without merging. Lawful third-party reuse remains governed by the applicable licenses.
